Junming Zhao

I'm a PhD candidate at UNSW Sydney, working in the Trustworthy Systems team led by Prof. Gernot Heiser. My research focuses on verifying a multithreaded network subsystem built on the seL4 secure microkernel, using Isabelle/HOL, rely-guarantee reasoning, and separation logic. I'm supervised by Dr. Rob Sison, Dr. Thomas Sewell, and Dr. Miki Tanaka.
